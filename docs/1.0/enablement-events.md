# Enablement Events

Enablement Events are training sessions, workshops, and learning activities that PSMs organize for their partner portfolio. Events can be one-time or recurring, with session-level tracking.

## Viewing Events

Navigate to **Engage > Enablement Events** to see upcoming and past events.

<!-- ![Enablement events calendar](images/enablement-events.png) -->

## Creating an Event

1. Click **New Event**
2. Enter the event name, description, and type
3. Set the date, time, and capacity
4. Add sessions if the event spans multiple days or topics
5. Click **Save**

## Event Sessions

An event can have multiple sessions. Each session has its own:

- Date and time
- Topic or focus area
- Instructor or facilitator
- Capacity limit

## Registrations

Partners and their contacts can register for events. Registrations can be:

- Created by the PSM on behalf of a contact
- Submitted by the partner through the [Partner Portal](portal-overview)

Track registration status (confirmed, waitlisted, attended, no-show) for each participant.

## Event Analytics

After an event, review attendance and engagement metrics:

- Registration vs attendance rate
- Partner distribution across sessions
- Feedback and ratings (if collected)

> [!TIP]
> Enablement event participation feeds into the partner scorecard under the engagement dimension.
