# Field Enablement

> [!NOTE]
> Available to: PSM, Manager, Administrator

Field Enablement helps PSMs map and manage their relationships across the partner's organization — tracking key contacts, influence patterns, and engagement depth.

## Influence Map

The influence map visualizes the breadth and depth of your engagement with a partner's team:

- **Breadth** — How many contacts across different roles and departments are you engaged with?
- **Depth** — How strong are those relationships? Are you engaging at decision-maker level?

<!-- ![Influence map](images/influence-map.png) -->

## Key Contacts

Manage the partner's contact directory:

- Add and edit contact information
- Assign roles and departments
- Track interaction history with each contact
- Flag key influencers and decision-makers

## Contact Interactions

Log interactions with partner contacts to build a history of engagement:

- Meeting notes
- Email exchanges
- Training sessions attended
- Event participation

> [!TIP]
> Consistent interaction logging gives you a clear picture of relationship health at QBR time and helps identify contacts who may be disengaging.

## Importing Contacts

Contacts can be imported in bulk via CSV upload. Navigate to the contact list and click **Import** to upload a formatted file.

> [!NOTE]
> Portal contacts (those with portal access) are managed separately under [User Management](user-management).
