# Deal Registration

Deal Registration allows partners to register new opportunities for approval by their PSM. This creates visibility into the partner's pipeline and enables co-selling coordination.

## How It Works

1. A partner submits a deal registration through the Partner Portal
2. The PSM receives a notification and reviews the submission
3. The PSM approves, requests changes, or declines the registration
4. Approved registrations are linked to the partner's opportunity pipeline

## Viewing Deal Registrations

Navigate to **Engage > Deal Registration** to see all registrations across your portfolio.

<!-- ![Deal registration list](images/deal-registration-list.png) -->

Registrations can be filtered by:

- Status (pending, approved, declined)
- Partner
- Date range
- Deal size

## Registration Details

Each registration includes:

- Customer name and contact
- Opportunity description and estimated value
- Expected close date
- Partner's proposed role and involvement
- Supporting notes

## Approval Workflow

When reviewing a registration:

1. Click on the pending registration
2. Review the details
3. Click **Approve**, **Request Changes**, or **Decline**
4. Add comments explaining your decision

> [!NOTE]
> Partners can also submit deal registrations through the [Partner Portal](portal-overview). They will see the status of their submissions in their portal dashboard.

## Linking to Opportunities

Approved deal registrations can be linked to opportunities in the pipeline. This connects the partner's co-sell activity to actual revenue tracking.
