# Launch Programs

> [!NOTE]
> Available to: CoE, Administrator (creation and management). PSM, Manager (viewing enrolled partners). Requires the `launch_programs_admin` or `launch_programs_user` feature flag to be active.

Launch Programs are structured programs designed to guide a cohort of partners through a product launch, solution go-to-market, or strategic initiative. CoE users create and manage programs, while PSMs can view their partners' enrollment and progress.

## How Launch Programs Work

A launch program defines:

- **Milestones** — Key checkpoints that participants must complete
- **Partners** — The partners participating in the program
- **Participants** — Specific contacts from each partner organization
- **Timeline** — Start date, end date, and milestone deadlines

## Creating a Launch Program

1. Navigate to **Strategy & CoE > Launch Programs**
2. Click **New Launch Program**
3. Enter the program name, description, and timeline
4. Add milestones with descriptions and due dates
5. Enroll partners and their participants

## Milestones

Milestones are the key checkpoints in a launch program:

- Each milestone has a title, description, and due date
- Milestone completions are tracked per partner
- Overdue milestones trigger notifications

## Tracking Progress

The program dashboard shows:

- Overall program progress
- Per-partner milestone completion
- Upcoming milestones and deadlines
- Feedback collected from participants

## Participant Feedback

Partners can submit feedback on the launch program through the portal. Feedback is collected per milestone and includes:

- Rating (satisfaction scale)
- Comments and suggestions
- Blockers or challenges encountered

> [!NOTE]
> Launch programs are visible to enrolled partners through the [Partner Portal](portal-overview). Partners can track their milestones and submit feedback directly.

## Date Changes

If program timelines need adjustment, date changes are tracked with a history log showing the original and modified dates, along with the reason for the change.
