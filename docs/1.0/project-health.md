# Project Health

> [!NOTE]
> Available to: PSM, Manager, Administrator. Requires the `project_health_user` or `project_health_admin` feature flag to be active.

The Project Health view provides visibility into a partner's active project portfolio — tracking delivery status, resource utilization, risks, and team composition.

## Project Overview

Navigate to **Engage > Project Health** to see all projects for a selected partner.

<!-- ![Project health dashboard](images/project-health.png) -->

Each project card shows:

- Project name and client
- Current status (active, at risk, completed)
- Start and end dates
- Team size and resource allocation
- Risk indicators

## Project Details

Click on a project to see its full details, including:

- Team composition and resource assignments
- Support case history
- Risk factors and mitigation notes
- Resource snapshots over time

## Risk Indicators

Partner Manager automatically flags projects that may be at risk based on:

- Resource utilization exceeding thresholds
- Approaching deadlines with incomplete milestones
- Support case volume spikes
- Team composition gaps

> [!NOTE]
> Risk indicators are computed by the Delivery Capability Risk Service and feed into the partner health score. See [Health Dashboard](health-dashboard) for how project health affects the overall partner assessment.

## Resource Snapshots

Resource snapshots capture point-in-time records of team allocation and utilization. These are used for trend analysis — you can see how a partner's delivery capacity has changed over time.
