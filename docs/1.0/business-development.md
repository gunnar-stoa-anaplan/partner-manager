# Business Development

> [!NOTE]
> Available to: PSM, Manager, Administrator

The Business Development module tracks lead sourcing, influenced pipeline, and development funding across your partner portfolio.

## Sourced Leads

Sourced leads are opportunities that partners have originated — deals that would not exist without the partner's involvement.

### Creating a Lead

1. Navigate to **Partner Management > Business Development > Sourced Leads**
2. Click **Create Lead**
3. Enter lead details: name, email, company, title, source, and notes
4. Assign to a partner (or leave unassigned for routing)

### Lead Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Unassigned** | New lead awaiting partner assignment |
| **Assigned** | Lead matched to a partner for follow-up |
| **Rejected** | Lead declined by the assigned partner or disqualified |

### Managing Leads

- Filter leads by status, partner, date range, or source
- Reassign leads between partners when needed
- Track conversion from lead to registered deal

## Influenced Leads

Influenced leads track pipeline where a partner played a supporting role — providing expertise, customer introductions, or implementation capacity — even though they did not originate the opportunity.

- View influenced pipeline by partner
- Track the influence type (technical validation, customer reference, implementation partner)
- Compare influenced vs. sourced ratios across the portfolio

## Development Funding

Track co-marketing and co-selling budget allocation across partners:

- View funding commitments by partner and program
- Monitor spend against allocated budgets
- Link funding to pipeline outcomes for ROI measurement

> [!TIP]
> Business Development data feeds into the [Revenue Analysis](revenue-analysis) views and the Pipeline tab on individual partner profiles. Keeping lead data current improves the accuracy of portfolio-level analytics.
