# Revenue Analysis

Revenue Analysis provides visibility into the partner's pipeline — from lead sourcing through opportunity progression to closed-won revenue.

## Pipeline Overview

Navigate to **Measure > Revenue Analysis** to see the revenue pipeline for your portfolio.

<!-- ![Revenue analysis](images/revenue-analysis.png) -->

The pipeline view shows:

- **Funnel visualization** — Leads, qualified opportunities, proposals, closed-won
- **Pipeline value** — Total and by stage
- **Sourced vs Influenced** — Revenue the partner originated vs revenue they contributed to
- **Win rate** — Close ratio for partner-involved opportunities

## Opportunity Tracking

Individual opportunities are tracked with:

- Customer name and account
- Estimated value and close date
- Stage in the sales cycle
- Partner's role (sourced, influenced, delivery)

## Opportunity Snapshots

Pipeline snapshots are taken periodically to capture the state of the pipeline at a point in time. This enables:

- Quarter-over-quarter comparison
- Pipeline velocity analysis
- Forecast accuracy tracking

## Revenue Goals

Revenue metrics are compared against the partner's fiscal-year goals (see [Goals & Targets](goals-and-targets)). The gap between actuals and targets is displayed prominently.

> [!TIP]
> Use revenue analysis data during QBR sessions to have data-driven pipeline discussions with your partners.
