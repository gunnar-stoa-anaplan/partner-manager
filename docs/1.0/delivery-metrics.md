# Delivery Metrics

> [!NOTE]
> Available to: PSM, Manager, Administrator

Delivery Metrics track the quality and efficiency of your partners' project delivery — success rates, resource utilization, and capacity trends.

## Key Metrics

Navigate to **Measure > Delivery Metrics** to see delivery performance across your portfolio.

<!-- ![Delivery metrics](images/delivery-metrics.png) -->

| Metric | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Project Success Rate** | Percentage of projects completed on time and within scope |
| **Resource Utilization** | How fully the partner's delivery team is allocated |
| **Active Projects** | Count of currently active projects |
| **Support Case Volume** | Number of open support cases across projects |

## Resource Snapshots

Resource snapshots capture point-in-time records of team composition and utilization. These are taken periodically and allow you to:

- Track how a partner's delivery capacity changes over time
- Identify partners that are over- or under-utilized
- Spot trends before they become problems

## Risk Assessment

The Delivery Capability Risk Service evaluates each partner's delivery portfolio and flags:

- Projects with high resource strain
- Partners with declining utilization trends
- Potential capacity gaps for upcoming projects

> [!NOTE]
> Delivery metrics feed into the partner scorecard and health score. A partner with consistently poor delivery metrics will see their health score decline, triggering alerts.
