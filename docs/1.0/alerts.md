# Alerts

Alerts are system-generated notifications that flag conditions requiring PSM attention. They are the "early warning system" that ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

## Alert Types

Partner Manager generates alerts for conditions such as:

- **Certification expiry** — A partner's certification is approaching expiration
- **Overdue tasks** — Tasks past their due date
- **Health decline** — A partner's health score has dropped below a threshold
- **Stalled progression** — A partner has been at the same path stage for too long
- **Pipeline risk** — Significant pipeline value has been lost or downgraded
- **Goal shortfall** — A partner is significantly behind on fiscal-year targets

## Viewing Alerts

Navigate to **Act > Alerts** to see all active alerts across your portfolio.

<!-- ![Alerts list](images/alerts.png) -->

Alerts can be filtered by:

- Severity (critical, warning, informational)
- Partner
- Alert type
- Date range

## Responding to Alerts

Each alert includes:

- The partner it relates to
- A description of the condition
- Suggested actions
- Link to the relevant detail page

Alerts can be acknowledged, deferred, or resolved.

> [!NOTE]
> Alerts are generated automatically by the Alert Generation Service based on configurable thresholds. Admins can adjust these thresholds under [Configuration](configuration).

## Alert History

Resolved alerts are archived but remain accessible for audit and trend analysis. You can review historical alert patterns to identify partners that consistently trigger the same types of alerts.
