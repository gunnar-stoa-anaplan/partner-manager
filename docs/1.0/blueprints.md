# Blueprints

Blueprints are structured learning paths that guide partners through a sequence of content — courses, documentation, hands-on labs, and assessments. They provide a clear roadmap for skill development.

## How Blueprints Work

A blueprint defines:

- **Outcomes** — What the partner will be able to do after completing the blueprint
- **Content Items** — The individual learning resources (videos, documents, labs, assessments)
- **Order** — The sequence in which content should be completed

## Viewing Blueprints

Navigate to **Engage > Blueprints** to see blueprints assigned to your partners.

<!-- ![Blueprint detail](images/blueprint-detail.png) -->

## Blueprint Enrollment

Partners are enrolled in blueprints by their PSM or through the portal. Once enrolled, the partner can track their progress through the content items.

## Progress Tracking

Each content item in a blueprint is tracked individually:

- **Not Started** — The partner hasn't begun this item
- **In Progress** — The partner has started but not completed
- **Completed** — The partner has finished the item

The overall blueprint progress is shown as a percentage.

## Outcomes

Blueprint outcomes are verified upon completion. They can feed into:

- Certification requirements
- Gate criteria for path advancement
- Competency matrix updates

> [!NOTE]
> Blueprints are created and managed by admins under [Blueprints Admin](blueprints-admin). PSMs can assign published blueprints to their partners.

## Partner Portal Access

Partners can view their assigned blueprints and track progress through the [Partner Portal](portal-overview). They see a clear view of what's been completed and what's next.
