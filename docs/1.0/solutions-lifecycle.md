# Solutions Lifecycle

The Solutions Lifecycle tracks partner-built IP and solutions through their development, validation, and go-to-market stages.

## Solution Stages

A solution progresses through defined stages:

1. **Ideation** — Partner has identified a solution concept
2. **Development** — Solution is being built
3. **Validation** — Solution is being tested and validated
4. **Published** — Solution is available for customer deployment
5. **Retired** — Solution is no longer actively maintained

## Viewing Solutions

Navigate to **Engage > Solutions Lifecycle** to see all partner solutions.

<!-- ![Solutions list](images/solutions-list.png) -->

## Creating a Solution

1. Click **New Solution**
2. Enter the solution name, description, and target market
3. Set the initial stage
4. Assign the partner
5. Click **Save**

## Stage Transitions

When a solution is ready to advance, update its stage. Every stage transition is recorded in the solution's history with a timestamp, providing a complete audit trail.

## Solution History

The stage history shows every transition a solution has gone through, including:

- Previous and new stage
- Date of transition
- Notes or comments

> [!TIP]
> Track solutions alongside certifications and project delivery to get a complete picture of a partner's capability development.
