# Idea Room

> [!NOTE]
> Available to: PSM, Manager, Administrator. Requires the `idea_room` feature flag to be active.

The Idea Room is a collaborative ideation workspace where teams brainstorm, organize, and refine ideas using AI-assisted tools and visual interfaces.

## Rooms

Idea Rooms are themed collaboration spaces where participants discuss topics with AI assistance.

### Creating a Room

1. Navigate to **Partner Management > Idea Room**
2. Click **Create Room**
3. Provide a title, description, and optional theme
4. Invite participants or leave the room open

### AI-Assisted Discussion

Each room includes an AI chat interface that helps facilitate ideation. The AI can:

- Suggest related concepts and connections
- Summarize discussion threads
- Challenge assumptions and offer alternative perspectives
- Help refine raw ideas into structured proposals

### Ideas List

Browse all ideas across rooms from the **Ideas** view. Ideas can be created within a room discussion or submitted independently. Each idea tracks its origin room, contributors, and status.

## Gallery Wall

The Gallery provides a visual asset library for ideation themes and templates:

- **Themes** — Pre-designed visual themes for rooms and whiteboards
- **Templates** — Reusable starting points for common ideation exercises
- **Publishing** — Share completed ideation artifacts with the broader team

Administrators can manage gallery themes and configure which templates are available.

## Whiteboard

The Whiteboard is a freeform drawing surface for visual thinking:

- **Configurable templates** with predefined writing areas and zones
- **Drawing tools** for sketching concepts, flows, and diagrams
- **Collaborative** — multiple participants can contribute to the same board

## Constellation

The Constellation view presents a visual graph of idea relationships:

- Ideas appear as nodes, connections as edges
- Cluster related concepts to identify themes
- Explore how ideas from different rooms intersect
- Discover unexpected connections across the portfolio

> [!TIP]
> Use the Constellation view during strategy sessions to identify patterns across multiple ideation threads. It's especially useful for spotting convergent thinking across different PSM portfolios.
